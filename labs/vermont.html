Vermont labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the Vermont Department of Health (VDH) Laboratory: (1) Drinking Water Testing / Homeowner kits — order kits for private drinking water (including the Homeowner Testing Package) via the VDH Drinking Water Testing page, online webstore, or kit order form (800-660-9997 / 802-338-4724; 359 South Park Drive, Colchester, VT); (2) Forms & Ordering + Sample Drop-off via Forms & Ordering Information and Sample Drop-off Information (Lab, Office of Local Health sites, or mail/ship within hold times). Verified for private well owners

VDH Laboratory — Drinking Water Testing / Homeowner kits

Drinking Water Testing · How to Test Your Drinking Water · Public Health Lab hub

The Health Department recommends testing private well or spring water with the Homeowner Testing Package every five years, and for bacteria every year (bacteria, inorganic chemicals, and gross alpha radiation). Kits are ordered through the Lab for the public. VDH Laboratory is green-highlighted on the certified PDF (accepts private samples) and is listed on the DEC landowner table with New Well Testing Yes.

Contacts (as printed on the kit order form / certified PDF): 359 South Park Drive, Colchester, VT 05446 · mailing PO Box 1125, Burlington, VT 05402-1125 · 802-338-4724 · 800-660-9997 (toll-free in Vermont) · fax 802-338-4706 · LAB ID VT 00020.

Fees: Confirm current amounts with the Lab — this directory does not republish the order-form price table.

Forms & Ordering + Sample Drop-off

Forms & Ordering Information · Online webstore (water and radon kits) · Sample Drop-off Information

Order by webstore, phone, mail, or fax using the drinking water and radon test kit order form. Drop off samples in-person at the Lab (Monday–Thursday 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; Fridays 7:45 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; closed state holidays), at participating Offices of Local Health, or mail/ship within the hold times in your kit instructions (some tests require cold samples and arrival within 22 hours).

Schedule note (retrieved 24 September 2026): State offices will not accept drinking water samples for courier pickup on Thursday, October 8, Friday, October 9, or Monday, October 12. The Lab will be closed Friday, October 9 (Professional Development Day) and Monday, October 12 (Indigenous Peoples’ Day).

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
