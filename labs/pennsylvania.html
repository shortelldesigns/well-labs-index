Pennsylvania labs

One laboratory is independently verified to take private-well samples from homeowners: Penn State’s Agricultural Analytical Services Laboratory. The other rows are commercial or independent laboratories transcribed from PA DEP’s drinking-water microbiology accreditation report dated 13 June 2024. We have not verified that those labs currently accept private-well samples. Call before you collect water.

Sources retrieved 29 August 2026. DEP private-well testing page: pa.gov/…/water-testing. Search current accreditation: PA Laboratory Accreditation Program.

What we could and could not verify

  • PA DEP’s water-testing page links to PDFs of labs that perform bacteriological and chemical testing. Direct HTTPS download of those PDFs from files.dep.state.pa.us failed with a TLS error from this build environment. WebFetch later retrieved the 13 June 2024 bacteriology report.
  • That report lists many municipal water-treatment-plant laboratories. Those were omitted here because this directory is for private well owners, and the report does not say those plants take homeowner samples.
  • Accreditation on this page is for drinking-water total coliform and/or E. coli. Metals, VOCs, and other chemicals are separate fields of accreditation. Use DEP’s search tool (matrix: Drinking Water) as Penn State Extension describes in Chain of Custody Water Testing (updated 29 May 2025).
  • Penn State Extension: be cautious of results from uncertified labs or from water-treatment salespeople. The Agricultural Analytical Services Lab does not provide chain-of-custody testing.

Verified for private well owners

Agricultural Analytical Services Laboratory (Penn State)

720 Tower Rd, University Park, PA 16802 · 814-863-0841 · aaslab@psu.edu
DEP Lab ID 14-00588. Hours listed by Penn State: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.

Penn State: the laboratory is accredited by PA DEP for drinking-water analysis. Drinking-water test kits are available at participating county Extension offices or directly from the lab (pre-addressed insulated mailer). Bacteria samples must reach the lab within 30 hours; ship Monday–Wednesday so the package does not sit over a weekend. Chain-of-custody is not offered here.

Sources: agsci.psu.edu/aasl/water-testing · Testing Your Drinking Water (2 June 2025) · How to use the kit.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
