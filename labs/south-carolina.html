South Carolina labs

The South Carolina Department of Environmental Services (SCDES) Office of Environmental Laboratory Certification maps every commercial environmental lab it certifies in CELMapp. The table below lists all 24 in-state commercial labs that hold SCDES Safe Drinking Water Act certification, from CELMapp data refreshed 10/10/2026; 15 are certified for total coliform/E. coli and 7 for nitrate. SCDES does not say which labs take private-well samples or what they charge. Call before you collect water. Test first.

Testing a private well in South Carolina: the SCDES kit

SCDES publishes a Well Water Quality Testing Services page for residential well owners. Pick up a sample kit at your local SCDES Regional Office or health department. There are two kits: one for coliform bacteria and one for metals and minerals. SCDES accepts samples on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and the invoice comes with your results. Don’t pay at drop-off.

  • Coliform bacteria test: $20 per test (total coliform; if positive, the same sample is tested for E. coli). Results come by mail in two to three weeks.
  • Metals and minerals analysis: $50 per test. SCDES says its lab is not accepting metals and minerals samples from June 1 to November 30, 2026; the December 1, 2026 restart date is tentative.
  • SCDES offers one free coliform test and one free metals and minerals analysis a year to people over 65 or on public assistance (bring proof). New permitted residential wells may get the first coliform test at no extra charge.
  • For volatile organics, herbicides, pesticides and other synthetic organics, SCDES says to use a commercial laboratory, such as one from the table below.
  • SCDES says it does not regulate private-well water quality and recommends that these tests not be used for real-estate transactions. Questions: toll-free 1-888-761-5989.
  • Private well owners can also apply to SCDES for a PFAS assessment through the same page.

Source: SCDES Well Water Quality Testing Services, read 3 November 2026. Fees and the metals pause are as SCDES printed them that day; check the page before you go.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
