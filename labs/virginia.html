Virginia labs

One path is independently verified for private wells: the Virginia Household Water Quality Program (VAHWQP) runs low-cost drinking-water clinics through local Virginia Cooperative Extension offices (kit pickup, sample day, analysis at Virginia Tech during scheduled clinics). VDH states it does not test private wells. DCLS states it does not provide private-well testing for homeowners. The table below lists in-state laboratories that appear on both DCLS’s VELAP Chapter 46 commercial directory (list dated 7/1/2026) and the Drinking Water Certified Labs Excel (current as of 8/1/2026). Neither roster labels private-well acceptance — call before you collect water.

Sources retrieved 11 September 2026. Private-well clinics: VAHWQP · Find your Extension office. VDH guidance: Private Well Water Testing · Private Well Program. Certified lab hub: DCLS Find a Lab · DCLS Environmental FAQ.

Verified for private well owners

Virginia Household Water Quality Program (VAHWQP) — Extension drinking water clinics

Start with your local Virginia Cooperative Extension office or the VAHWQP program page · Coordinator contact on VAHWQP: wellwater@vt.edu · (540) 231-9058

VAHWQP (retrieved 11 September 2026) provides affordable, confidential water testing programs for private wells, springs, and cisterns through local Extension offices in around 70 Virginia counties each year. A clinic includes local kit pick-up, a sample collection day with local drop-off, sample transport to Virginia Tech for analysis, and results about 4–5 weeks later (sometimes with a local results meeting). Campus labs are not equipped to handle individual analysis requests outside scheduled clinics — contact your Extension office to express interest. VDH’s Private Well Water Testing page also points homeowners to VAHWQP clinics and to DCLS-listed commercial labs for containers and instructions. Confirm clinic dates, kit instructions, and current fees with your local Extension office before you collect a sample. Prices intentionally omitted from this directory.

Source: VAHWQP · Extension offices · VDH Private Well Water Testing · VDH Private Well Program (VDH does not test private wells).

DCLS — not available for private homeowners

The Division of Consolidated Laboratory Services Environmental FAQ states DCLS does not provide consultation on private well testing or testing for homeowners. DCLS drinking-water analysis supports public water supply / regulated waterworks. Do not treat DCLS as a verified private-well lab. Source: DCLS Environmental FAQ.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
