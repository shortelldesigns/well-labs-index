Idaho labs

The table below lists 8 laboratories from the DHW Idaho Certified Laboratories roster on the Drinking Water and Private Wells hub (retrieved 3 October 2026). Verified for private well owners

Free Idaho Private Well Water Testing (Available Fall 2026) — Idaho Health Tools

Drinking Water and Private Wells · Idaho Health Tools — Idaho Private Well Water Testing

DHW states it is offering free private well water testing to help Idaho residents understand drinking-water quality. Private wells are not regulated like public water systems. Contaminants named on the hub for this program include nitrate, fluoride, arsenic, lead, uranium, manganese, chromium, nickel, copper, zinc, selenium, cadmium, and barium. Residents can sign up through Idaho Health Tools to request a free testing kit mailed to the home. Results include guidance on what they mean and recommended next steps. The hub notes processing may take up to two months and that residents needing testing sooner may wish to test independently.

Availability note (as printed tonight): Hub title says Available Fall 2026. Idaho Health Tools product page printed Currently sold out, additional tests will be available soon and Only one test kit per household can be ordered every five years. Confirm current status on the official Health Tools page before relying on this path.

Agency contacts (as printed): Environmental Health Program — email EH@dhw.idaho.gov · phone 1-800-445-8647.

Fees: Program described as free on the DHW hub / Health Tools page when kits are available — confirm current status on the official page. Independent lab fees are omitted from this directory.

Idaho Bureau of Labs — certified list + private-well / citizen-science path

Drinking Water Testing and Certification · Private Wells hub (Certified Laboratories table)

Address and phone as printed on the DHW Certified Laboratories table: 2220 Old Penitentiary Rd., Boise · 208-334-2235. The Drinking Water Testing and Certification page states IBL analyzes environmental samples and certifies environmental labs; call 208-334-2235 with questions about sample submission; consult the Sampling and Submission Guide; proper submission forms must accompany samples. The private-wells hub states private well owners utilizing IBL as their testing laboratory can participate in citizen science by granting permission on IBL’s submission form to display results on the Idaho Private Well Drinking Water Map. Call to confirm packages, holding times, and fees.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
