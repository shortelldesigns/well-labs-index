Mississippi labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the Mississippi State Department of Health (MSDH): (1) Private Well Water Testing — MSDH tests private wells for bacteriological contamination only (minerals, chemicals, or other contaminants go through a private laboratory); (2) Apply online for private well water sampling — the Division of On-site Wastewater takes a sample from a single private well for bacteriological testing. Verified for private well owners

MSDH Private Well Water Testing — bacteriological only

Private Well Water Testing · Private Wells hub · Disinfection Procedure PDF

The Mississippi State Department of Health tests private wells for bacteriological contamination only. Testing for mineral content, chemicals or other contaminants should be done through a private laboratory. This is a sampling service for private water wells only. For homes connected to public water systems, contact your water supply operator, or MSDH’s Bureau of Public Water Supply.

Agency (as printed in MSDH footers): Mississippi State Department of Health · 570 East Woodrow Wilson Dr · Jackson, MS 39216 · 866-HLTHY4U. Certification-status questions for participating labs: 601-576-7582 (as printed on the Participating Labs page).

Fees: Confirm current amounts with MSDH / Division of On-site Wastewater — this directory does not republish fee lines.

Apply online — Private Well Sample

Online application (Private Well Sample)

The Department will take a sample of the water from your well and test it for bacteriological contamination. The application is for a single well. The form states that minerals, chemicals, or other contaminants require a private lab and links to the In-State Participating Labs list. The Division of On-site Wastewater will not take water samples for homes connected to public water systems — contact the Bureau of Public Water Supply for public-system concerns.

In-state participating laboratories (MSDH Water Supply Certification)

MSDH lists these labs under Water Supply Certification: In-State Participating Labs (also linked from the private-well page as laboratories participating in MSDH’s Drinking Water Lab Certification Program). Certification status can change — call MSDH at 601-576-7582 with questions. Call each lab to confirm they will take your private-well sample, which analytes they offer, shipping instructions, and current fees. 0 of 6 rows have independently verified private-well packages tonight.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
