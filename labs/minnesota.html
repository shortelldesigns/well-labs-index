Minnesota labs that accept private well samples

Transcribed from the Minnesota Department of Health PDF Accredited Labs in Minnesota Accepting Drinking Water Samples from Private Well Users, dated July 2026. MDH built this list for private well owners. We did not add laboratories from other directories.

Source retrieved 29 August 2026. Official file: labmap.pdf. MDH: these laboratories held valid accreditation when the map was created. Appearance here is not an endorsement.

How to read the table

Yes
MDH marked the lab for that analyte.
Yes*
Sample is subcontracted to a laboratory accredited for that analyte.
—
That column was blank on the MDH table.
Mail / drop-off
Unknown. MDH’s drop-off star icon did not extract from the PDF. Call the lab. Ask for bottles and hold times before you sample.

For other contaminants, MDH: search Accredited Laboratories → Customized Searches → Program: Safe Drinking Water Program → analyte → check “Accepts samples from private homeowners.”

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
