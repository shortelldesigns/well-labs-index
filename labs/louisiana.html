Louisiana labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH): (1) the Private Well Owner Network sample path — order online, then drop off or arrange sampling through Office of Public Health laboratories and parish health units; (2) Water Test, Inc., which the official microbiology certified list marks PRIVATE WELLS ONLY. The table below lists 9 in-state laboratories useful to private-well owners from LDH’s certified microbiology list (March 2026), Sample Drop-Off Locations PDF, and chemistry certified roster / homeowner analyte matrix (retrieved 2 October 2026). Municipal public-water-system plant labs are omitted. Call before you collect water. Test first.

Verified for private well owners

LDH Private Well Owner Network — Order testing + OPH / parish health unit sample path

Private Well Owner Network · Order Private Water Well Testing · Private Well Testing Information · Sample Drop-Off Locations (PDF) · Sample Drop Off Sites map

Private water wells are not regulated under the Safe Drinking Water Act the way public systems are. LDH’s Private Well Owner Network hosts sampling and stewardship resources. Homeowners can order private water well testing online and use LDH Office of Public Health laboratories and parish health units listed on the official Sample Drop-Off Locations PDF (laboratory drop-off Monday–Thursday by 2:00 pm excluding holidays; parish health unit times vary — call ahead).

Agency contacts (as printed): Private Well Initiative — email PWI@LA.GOV · phone 1-888-293-7020. Laboratory Certification / OPH Laboratory: (225) 219-5200.

Fees: Confirm current amounts on the Private Well Testing Information page — this directory does not republish fee lines in the table.
