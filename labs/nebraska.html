Nebraska labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) Public Health Environmental Laboratory (NPHEL): (1) private citizen bacteria / coliform kits (Presence/Absence or Numerical Count) ordered via the Private Citizen Water Test Kit Request Form or Customer Service 402-471-3935 — samples must use NPHEL kits, be collected and mailed the same day for coliform, arrive within 30 hours, and ship Monday–Thursday; (2) private citizen nitrate kits on the same form / Customer Service path (DWEE private-well sampling article corroborates the DHHS online kit path). The table below lists 3 in-state commercial / reciprocity laboratories from DHHS’s official certified labs.pdf (linked from Lab Certification Requirements; PDF metadata ModDate 22 May 2024; retrieved 19 September 2026). The Central District Health Department and Metropolitan Utilities Districts entries on that PDF are omitted from the commercial table. Call before you collect water.

Verified for private well owners

NPHEL private citizen bacteria / coliform kits

Private Citizen Water Test Kit Request Form · Customer Service / order kits · Public Health Environmental Lab

The Private Citizen form offers Type of Bacteria Kit options: Presence/Absence or Numerical Count. It states coliform samples must be collected and mailed the same day; deliver or mail samples Monday through Thursday; beware of state and federal holidays; Sample over 30 hours old will not be tested. Unused kits have a 90-day return policy as printed on the form.

Customer Service instructions state samples must be collected in sample kits supplied by the Nebraska Public Health Environmental Laboratory; sampling instructions are included with kits; order online (Private Clients or Companies) or call 402-471-3935, Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. except holidays.

Contacts (as printed on the Public Health Lab hub): Main laboratory 402-471-2122 · sample kits press 2 (471-3935) · private well results interpretation press 3 (471-4982) · Mailing address 3701 South 14th St., Lincoln, Nebraska 68502. Lab Certification Manager Tara Wulfekoetter 402-471-8407 (for certification questions).

Fees: Confirm current amounts with NPHEL — this directory does not republish a fee table.

NPHEL private citizen nitrate kits (+ DWEE guidance)

Private Citizen Water Test Kit Request Form · DWEE: Sample private drinking water wells

The same Private Citizen form includes a Nitrate kit quantity field. DWEE’s private-well sampling article states well owners can request sample kits from Nebraska DHHS online and that the lab tests for the presence of coliform bacteria; it also discusses nitrate risks (including not boiling high-nitrate water) and links the DHHS kit path and NPHEL price list.

Confirm which parameters are available for private citizens, how to submit, hold times, and current fees before you collect a sample.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
