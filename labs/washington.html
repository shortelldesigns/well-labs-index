Washington labs

Jump to the 47 Washington labs

The Washington State Department of Ecology Lab Accreditation Program accredits the labs that test drinking water in Washington. The table below lists all 47 Washington labs on Ecology’s drinking-water list by county, current as of 11/09/2026. 36 are accredited for total coliform/E. coli and 29 for nitrate, the two tests the Department of Health (DOH) says private well owners should run every year. 39 of them also appear on DOH’s private-well Nitrate and Coliform Testing Labs list (331-798, 3/27/2026). Neither agency says what each lab charges or confirms that it takes your sample. Call before you collect water. Test first.

Testing a private well in Washington

Washington DOH says that if you own a private well, testing your water is your job. Ecology says: “just contact an accredited lab,” and adds that some counties offer water testing through the local health department or district.

  • What to test: DOH recommends testing every year for coliform bacteria and nitrate. If nitrate is 5 mg/L or higher, you may want to retest in six months. DOH also suggests testing for arsenic at least twice while you own the well, once in summer and once in winter.
  • Cost: DOH’s 3/27/2026 lab list says nitrate and coliform tests cost $21–$55 depending on the lab. Prices for individual labs are not published. Ask the lab.
  • Free testing in two areas: If you live within a specific boundary of the Lower Yakima Valley or Western Benton County, DOH says you may qualify for free nitrate and coliform testing. Check with the DOH Well Water Sampling and Site Assessment form.
  • Buying or selling a home: DOH says the county health or planning department, or the lender, may require well-water results. Contact your local health jurisdiction for the tests it requires.
  • If coliform is present or nitrate is over 10 mg/L, DOH says to contact your local health department for advice (Private Wells: Information for Owners 331-349).

Sources: DOH Testing Your Water and Ecology Testing drinking water, both read 10 November 2026. Price range from DOH 331-798 (3/27/2026).

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
