Tennessee labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the Tennessee Department of Health (TDH) Division of Laboratory Services: (1) Environmental Microbiology accepts samples from private wells for Total Coliform / E. coli and provides sterile bottles with forms; (2) the Chemistry Laboratory lists private wells among typical sample sources for environmental / drinking-water chemistry — confirm parameters and bottle orders before you collect. The table below lists 10 in-state commercial / independent / university laboratories from TDEC’s Commercial Laboratories Certified for Drinking Water Analysis PDF dated 03/17/2026. Military / federal plant labs and all out-of-state labs are omitted. Call before you collect water.

Verified for private well owners

TDH Division of Laboratory Services — Environmental Microbiology (private wells)

TDH Division of Laboratory Services · Williamson County flyer (TDH contacts)

TDH states the Environmental Microbiology Unit performs bacteriological analyses on water samples from both public and private water systems, and that Environmental Microbiology accepts samples from private wells (among other sources). Tests include Total Coliform / E. coli by presence/absence or quantitative means. The lab provides sterile bottles with required forms for water sampling.

Contact: Kristin Dunaway, Supervisor, Environmental Microbiology · Kristin.Dunaway@tn.gov · 615-262-6337.

Nashville addresses (as printed on TDH lab page): carriers (FedEx/UPS): TDH Laboratory Services, 630 Hart Lane, Nashville, TN 37216 · USPS: TDH Laboratory Services, P.O. Box 305130, Nashville, TN 37230. A Williamson County flyer also lists supply-request contacts TDOH-ENVLogin.Health@tn.gov or 615-262-6337 for testing provided by TDH Division of Laboratory Services at 630 Hart Lane.

Fees: Confirm current amounts with TDH — this directory does not republish a fee table.

TDH Division of Laboratory Services — Chemistry Laboratory

TDH Division of Laboratory Services

TDH states the Chemistry Laboratory focuses on monitoring environmental and drinking water, and that samples tested are typically sourced from lakes, streams, rivers, ponds, open wells, private wells, unprotected springs, or sources with visible evidence of contamination.

For more information or help with bottle orders: TDOH-EnvLogin.Health@TN.gov or Chemistry Laboratory Manager 615-262-6346.

Confirm which parameters are available for private-well owners, how to submit, and current fees before you collect a sample.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
