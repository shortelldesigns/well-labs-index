Maine labs

One laboratory is independently verified to accept private-well drinking-water samples: the Maine Health and Environmental Testing Laboratory (HETL) in Augusta. The other rows are in-state commercial drinking-water laboratories transcribed from Maine CDC’s commercial labs PDF dated 5/16/2026 — the same PDF Maine’s private-well page links as “Find a lab that is certified to test your well water.” We have not verified that those other labs currently accept private-well samples. Call before you collect water.

Sources retrieved 4 September 2026. Official list: In-State Commercial Drinking Water Labs (PDF), linked from Maine Laboratory Accreditation and Private Well Water. HETL: Environmental Chemistry.

What we could and could not verify

  • The commercial PDF lists 12 in-state laboratories with lab code, name, website, city, and telephone. Street addresses are not printed — address is unknown on every row.
  • Specialty notes printed on the PDF (PFAS Only, Bacteria only) are kept in the Notes column. Labs are accredited by test; confirm current accreditation with the lab or the Laboratory Accreditation Officer at (207) 287-2070.
  • Maine CDC’s private-well page and accreditation page both point well owners to this commercial PDF. Appearance on it is not an endorsement and is not, by itself, verification of homeowner sample acceptance.
  • The PDF footer recommends checking bacteria holding times before mailing samples. Many laboratories offer statewide service — ask. Prices are intentionally omitted.
  • A complete “All Accredited Laboratories” Excel file exists separately; this directory transcribes only the in-state commercial drinking-water list.

Verified for private well owners

Health and Environmental Testing Laboratory (HETL)

Augusta, ME · (207) 287-2727 · Environmental Chemistry

On Maine CDC’s In-State Commercial Drinking Water Labs list (5/16/2026): Lab Code HETL. Maine CDC’s Environmental Chemistry page (retrieved 4 September 2026) states the lab tests drinking water from public utilities and from private wells for safety and contaminants. Street address and kit / drop-off / shipping instructions are not copied here — call the laboratory before you collect a sample. Prices are intentionally omitted.

Source: maine.gov … /environmental-chemistry · commercial labs PDF · Testing Your Wellwater.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
