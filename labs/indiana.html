Indiana labs

One laboratory is independently verified to accept private-well drinking-water samples from citizens: the Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) Laboratories in Indianapolis. The other rows are in-state laboratories transcribed from IDEM’s Indiana Certified Drinking Water Laboratories PDF revised 11/7/2025, from the section labeled “Labs that accept samples from the public.” Municipal utilities that appear in that public-accepting section are included. Appearance on the list is not an endorsement. Call before you collect water. Private-well acceptance beyond the PDF’s “accept samples from the public” wording is not independently verified except for IDOH.

Sources retrieved 5 September 2026. Official list: Indiana Certified Drinking Water Laboratories (PDF). Private-well kits: IDOH Laboratory — Well Water. Context: IDOH EPH — Well Water Quality and Testing · Drinking Water Laboratory Certification · IDEM Laboratories.

What we could and could not verify

  • The IDEM PDF separates labs that accept samples from the public from labs that do not. This directory transcribes only the public-accepting section (49 laboratories). The non-public section is not tabulated — see the PDF.
  • Municipal utilities listed in the public-accepting section are kept (unlike Ohio/Michigan pages, where municipal plants were omitted because those lists did not claim public acceptance).
  • Except for IDOH Laboratories, we have not independently verified private-well / homeowner acceptance beyond the PDF’s public-samples wording. Call before you collect.
  • The PDF text layer is multi-column and messy. City of Goshen Water Lab and Elkhart Public Works & Utilities Laboratory both print lab ID M-20-01; Pace Analytical Services, LLC (Indianapolis) prints only C-49-06 (same C-id string as EMSL). Kept as printed. Five labs have no website on the PDF (marked unknown).
  • Certification summaries may truncate long analyte lists with “…”. Prices are intentionally omitted even when the IDOH well-water page shows dollar amounts. Order IDOH kits via Access Indiana (link the IDOH page; kit process details beyond that page are not invented).

Verified for private well owners

Indiana Dept. of Health Laboratories

550 West 16th St, Ste B, Indianapolis, IN 46202 · (317) 921-5874 · IDOH Well Water testing

On IDEM’s certified list (revised 11/7/2025): Lab IDs M-IN-00 / C-IN-00; Marion County; certified for total coliform/E.coli, Enumeration of E. coli, Heterotrophic plate count, arsenic, lead, copper, fluoride, nitrate, nitrite, radionuclides. IDOH’s Laboratory well-water page (retrieved 5 September 2026) states the lab offers testing for private citizens using well water for drinking; recommends annual bacteria and nitrate, and at least every 5 years fluoride, arsenic, lead, and copper (or after major maintenance). Order kits via Access Indiana (linked from that page). Shipping address printed on the page: IDOH Laboratory Suite B, 550 W 16th St, Indianapolis, IN 46202. Prices are intentionally omitted.

Source: in.gov/health/laboratories/testing/well-water/ · IDEM certified-labs PDF · Well Water Quality and Testing.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
