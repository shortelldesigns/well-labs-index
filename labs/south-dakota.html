South Dakota labs

One path is independently verified for private well owners: the South Dakota Department of Health Public Health Laboratory / Environmental Health Laboratory accepts private-person testing via kits from DOH or special locations (County Extension offices also distribute bottles). The table below lists 2 in-state commercial laboratories from DANR’s Laboratory Certification roster. The State Health Laboratory (Pierre) is documented only in the verified path below — not as a commercial row. Sioux Falls Public Health Laboratory is a municipal/public health lab documented separately. Out-of-state labs on the DANR page are omitted from the main table. Call before you collect water.

Verified for private well owners

SD DOH Public Health Laboratory / Environmental Health Laboratory — private-person kits

Environmental Testing · Test a Private Well (sample bottle order) · Environmental Testing Fees · DANR Private Well Sampling

DOH states: if you wish to test a water source through the Environmental Health Laboratory, you need to get a testing kit from the Department of Health or a special location. Once you have the kit, follow the instructions on the kit. DANR offers helpful information about private well testing.

The sample bottle order page states bottles include complete instructions for collecting and submitting water samples, that fee information for the various tests is included, and that sample bottles and instructions are also available from County Extension offices.

South Dakota State Health Laboratory (as printed on DANR Laboratory Certification): Laboratory Building, 615 East Fourth Street, Pierre, South Dakota 57501 · 605-773-3368 · Contact person: Rea Riggle. Certified for: Inorganics, SOC's, VOC's, Chlorite, HAA5, Perchlorate, TTHM's, Microbiology, E.Coli enumeration, Chlorite.

DOH contact (Environmental Testing / bottle order pages): Rea Riggle, Laboratory Deputy Director and Environmental Health Supervisor · 615 E 4th Street, Pierre, SD 57501 · Rea.Riggle@state.sd.us · local 605-773-3368 · toll-free 1-800-738-2301 (in SD only) · fax 605-773-6129. Bottle-order questions may also go to Kristy Deal (kristy.deal@state.sd.us) at the same phone.

Fees: See the official Environmental Testing Fees page and the fee information included with bottle kits. Confirm current amounts with the lab — this directory does not republish a fee table.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
