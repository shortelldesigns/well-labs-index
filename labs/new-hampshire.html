New Hampshire labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the New Hampshire Department of Health and Human Services (NHDHHS) Public Health Laboratories Water Analysis Laboratory (WAL): (1) Homeowner Water Testing — comprehensive fee-for-service testing for analytes in private wells, with a full report and interpretation (WAL Program; contact 603-271-3445 / Waterlab@dhhs.nh.gov; 29 Hazen Drive, Concord, NH); (2) Home Owner Container Request via the NHDES DES Onestop HOBottles form (options include Standard Analysis, Drinking Water Bacteria, NH Well Water Test For Home Buyers, Standard Analysis Plus Radon, Radon Only, Arsenic Only, and others). The table below lists 9 laboratories from NHDES’s official labs-private-wells.pdf (“Water Testing Guidance for New Hampshire Private Well Users”; document header April 2026; PDF metadata ModDate 20 April 2026; lab-list footer updated 9/20/2024; retrieved 20 September 2026) that NHDES states serve New Hampshire private well users. NHDHHS Public Health Laboratories also appears on that PDF and is covered in the verified section above (not duplicated in the commercial table). Call before you collect water.

Verified for private well owners

NHDHHS WAL — Homeowner Water Testing

Water Analysis Laboratory Program · WAL contact · What’s in your Water

The WAL Program states it provides comprehensive testing for analytes in private wells of New Hampshire homeowners on a fee-for-service basis. Upon completion, homeowners receive a full report with an interpretation of results; staff can provide an unbiased assessment and further information on references and treatment options. WAL offers microbiology (total coliform and E. coli), inorganic chemistry, organic chemistry, and radiochemistry (primarily uranium and radon).

Contacts (as printed on the WAL contact page): 29 Hazen Drive, Concord NH 03301 · Waterlab@dhhs.nh.gov · 603-271-3445.

Fees: Confirm current amounts with WAL — this directory does not republish a fee table.

Schedule note (as printed on the WAL program page, retrieved 20 September 2026): WAL will not be accepting bacteria samples on Friday, September 25. Please plan accordingly.

NHDES Home Owner Container Request (kits / bottles)

Home Owner Container Request

Request homeowner test containers through DES Onestop. Form options verified on the page include: Standard Analysis; Radiological Analysis; Volatile Organic Chemicals; Drinking Water Bacteria; Basic Analysis; Swimming Water Bacteria; Suspected Sewage Surface Water; Fluoride; NH Well Water Test For Home Buyers; Standard Analysis Plus Radon; Radon Only; Arsenic Only; Lead Only (for service line ID); and Other. Confirm which kit matches NHDES’s recommended private-well panel, how to submit, hold times, and current fees before you collect a sample.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
