Montana labs

Two paths are independently verified for private well owners through the Montana Department of Public Health and Human Services (DPHHS) State Environmental Laboratory: (1) Private Well Testing — Chemistry (Basic Screen, metals, anions, organics packages on the official form); (2) Private Well Testing — Microbiology (bacteria-only form). The table below lists 15 unique Montana laboratory locations named on DPHHS’s Water Laboratory Certification Program page (retrieved 1 October 2026). That certification authorizes labs to analyze public drinking-water supplies; private-well acceptance is not stated per commercial or municipal row. Call before you collect water. Test first.

Verified for private well owners

DPHHS State Environmental Laboratory — Private Well Testing (Chemistry)

Environmental Laboratory hub · Chemistry Testing for Private Well Sample Submission Form (PDF)

The Montana State Environmental Laboratory (DPHHS) publishes a private-well chemistry sample submission form. Packages named on the form include a Basic Screen (coliform/E. coli presence-absence, nitrate + nitrite, specific conductivity), Metals Screen, Anion Screen, VOC, pesticide, herbicide, and Full Well Screen options. Samples must be paid in advance. Bacteria samples must reach the laboratory within 48 hours of collection.

Lab (as printed on forms / certification page): State of Montana Environmental Laboratory · 1400 Broadway, Room B-206 · Helena, MT 59601 · mail P.O. Box 4369, Helena, MT 59604 · (406) 444-3444 · toll-free (800) 821-7284.

Fees: Confirm current amounts with the Environmental Laboratory — this directory does not republish fee lines (fee schedule PDF linked under Official tools).

DPHHS State Environmental Laboratory — Private Well Testing (Microbiology)

Bacteria Testing for Private Well Sample Submission Form (PDF)

The same laboratory publishes a bacteria-only private-well form. Tests named on the form include Total Coliform presence/absence, Total Coliform count, iron bacteria, and sulfur bacteria. Keep the sample cool and return within 48 hours. Same Helena contact and delivery options as the chemistry form (mail carrier, laboratory courier, or hand delivery to the Cogswell Building loading dock).

Montana laboratories (DPHHS Water Laboratory Certification Program)

DPHHS lists these Montana locations under the Water Laboratory Certification Program (also out-of-state certified labs — see the official page; omitted here). Certification status can change — certification questions: (406) 444-5261 (Jill Cohenour, Chemistry Certification Officer, as printed). Call each lab to confirm they will take your private-well sample, which analytes they offer, shipping instructions, and current fees. Only the DPHHS Environmental Laboratory row is independently verified for private-well packages tonight.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
