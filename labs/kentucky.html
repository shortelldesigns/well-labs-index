Kentucky labs

Two paths are independently verified for private wells: (1) HydroAnalytical (WKU) offers a well water package — metals, anions, pH, total coliform, and E. coli; (2) Western KY Regional Lab at the Marshall County Health Department confirms private well/cistern bacterial testing, and many local health departments describe similar coliform sampling — ask yours. The table below lists 16 in-state commercial, independent, and regional laboratories from Kentucky DOW’s 2025 KY Certified Drinking Water Laboratory List, Revised 4/28/2025 (Greenup CHD mirror; official Documents path was unavailable at retrieve time). Municipal plant labs and state government labs are omitted. Commercial private-well acceptance is unknown except where noted — call before you collect water.

Verified for private well owners

HydroAnalytical — well water package

Well water information · Contact · (270) 745-5287

PDF mailing address (as printed): 1906 College Heights Blvd Bowling Green, KY 42101. Lab street (from hydroanalytical.com): 2413 Nashville Road, Ste. 100, Bowling Green, KY 42101.

The well water package includes a metals suite, anions suite, pH, total coliform, and E. coli. HydroAnalytical’s about page states sample collection and sample pickup are offered — confirm current drop-off / collection options with the lab. DOW and UK Extension both tell well owners to use a state-certified laboratory. Prices intentionally omitted.

Source: hydroanalytical.com/well-water-information · about · DOW list (KY00035).

Western KY Regional Lab — private well bacterial testing (Marshall County Health Department)

Marshall County water testing · (270) 252-2702 · 267 Slickback Rd Benton, KY 42025

Marshall County Health Department confirms bacterial analysis (total coliform and E. coli) for private wells and cisterns through Western KY Regional Lab (KY00090). Marshall County’s page lists a fee; fees vary by county — confirm locally. This directory does not publish a statewide price.

Local health departments — ask about private-well bacterial sampling

Ask your local county/district health department whether they collect private-well bacterial samples. Services and fees vary. Finder: KHDA Find Your Local Health Department · CHFS links: Local Health Departments. Example of how an LHD describes the service: Greenup CHD water-testing laboratories. After floods, DOW also points owners to the local health department: DOW owner assist.

Methodology and disclosure · State directory

This site is not a government agency and does not certify laboratories. Listings are transcribed from official state and federal sources cited on each page. Accreditation, phone numbers, and sample-acceptance policies change. Confirm with the laboratory and the state certification program before you ship a sample.

Private wells are not regulated under the federal Safe Drinking Water Act in the way public water systems are. You are responsible for testing.
