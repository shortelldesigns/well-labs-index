Testing Private Wells

Sample Bottle Order Form

To request sample bottles for testing of water supplies, please complete and submit the following form, indicating the number of bottles needed for each test specified.

Bottles include complete instructions for collecting and submitting water samples for testing. Fee information for the various tests is included. Sample bottles and instructions are also available from County Extension offices.

Questions can be directed to Rea Riggle or Kristy Deal at 605-773-3368
