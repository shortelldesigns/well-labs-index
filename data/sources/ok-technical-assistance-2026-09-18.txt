Myths The Little Black Box A common misconception about most laboratories is that the laboratory puts one sample in a box and obtains immense amounts of data within an hour. Unlike our beloved TV shows and movies, that is not the case. Every sample must be treated uniquely based on its matrix and the type of analysis requested. Samples take time to prepare before they can be analyzed, require a very specific time to analyze, followed by quality assurance review by analysts and managers.
