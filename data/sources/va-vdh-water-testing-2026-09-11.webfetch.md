# Archive note — VDH Private Well Water Testing
URL: https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/environmental-health/water-testing/
Retrieved: 2026-09-11 via WebFetch (curl returned HTTP 403)

Key facts transcribed for Well Labs Index (not a full HTML mirror):
- VDH points private well owners to DCLS VELAP accredited/certified laboratory listings.
- Most commercial laboratories can provide sample containers, preservatives, and collection instructions.
- Virginia Household Water Quality Program (Virginia Cooperative Extension) offers low-cost annual water well testing / drinking water clinics at various locations throughout Virginia.
- For interpreting results: ask the lab, ask Local Health Department (LDH), or use Ohio State Water Test Interpretation Tool (linked from VDH page).
- Contaminant topic pages linked: Arsenic, Bacteria, Lead, Nitrate, Radionuclides in private wells.
