Find a Lab

The Division of Consolidated Laboratory Services maintains an updated list of accredited and certified laboratories.

  • Accredited Commercial Laboratories under 1VAC30-46 with accreditation detail

    • SUGGESTED USE: Use the FILTER [arrow in column header] to select a desired parameter. Examples:

      • Selecting "Arsenic" in column I and "Non-Potable Water in Column F displays all labs with this matrix/analyte combination. Adding additional selections, such as preferred method or the state where laboratory is located, will further select the output.
      • Selecting a laboratory by name or ID number to display all accreditations or certifications held by the laboratory as of the date of the report.
