Wells

Water utilities are regulated by the Safe Drinking Water Act to test and treat public water supplies, but private well owners must essentially act as their own utility operator. This means that you have to maintain your own system, monitor your drinking water, and address any problems that arise. To start this process, you should get acquainted with your system and routinely test your water.

Notice to Private Well Owners

Water utilities are regulated by the EPA Safe Drinking Water Act to test and treat public water supplies, but private well owners are responsible to maintain their well and test the water quality. This means that you have to maintain and protect your well, test  well  water, and install and maintain any necessary water treatment/filtration. To start this process, you should get acquainted with your system and routinely test your water.
