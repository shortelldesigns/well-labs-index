The Safe Drinking Water Act specifies categories of federally regulated analytes. Laboratories certified to test an analyte group are marked with an “X,”
laboratories certified to test for some but not all analytes in a group are marked as “Partial.” Listings with an EPA marking provides the number of the testing
method used. Scope sheets are detailed listings of tests labs are certified for.

Note: Laboratories may be added to or deleted from this list at any time.

Laboratories highlighted in green accept private samples. Some laboratories that generally accept private samples may specifically not accept private PFAS
samples or may analyze them by a noncertified method. Please contact the individual laboratory for specifics.

For any questions, please contact the Laboratory Certification Officer by e-mail at SDWAcert@vermont.gov


Abbreviation              Explanation
BACT (QUAL)               Qualitative (presence/absence) analysis of total coliform bacteria and E. coli
BACT (QUANT, LT2)         Quantitative analysis of total coliform bacteria, E. coli, Cryptosporidium and
                          Giardia
IOC                       Inorganic chemicals (ex. metals, nitrate and nitrite)
ASB                       Asbestos
HAA5                      Haloacetic acids
THMs                      Trihalomethanes
VOC                       Volatile organic compounds
SOC A                     Synthetic organic chemicals sub-group A (EDB and DCBP)
SOC B                     Synthetic organic chemicals sub-group B (Carbamates)
SOC C                     Synthetic organic chemicals sub-group C (Herbicides)
SOC D                     Synthetic organic chemicals sub-group D (Pesticides, phthalates, PCBs)
PFAS                      Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances
TABLE WSR                 Water supply rule contaminants for non-community water systems
Partial                   Indicates that the laboratory is certified for some, but not all, regulated
                          analytes within the group
