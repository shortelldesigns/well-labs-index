Full-service environmental laboratory of Montana DPHHS. Serves general public and agencies.

Private Well Testing forms with collection instructions:
- Private Well Testing- Chemistry: Packages are available to screen for metals, nutrients, anions, organics, and more.
- Private Well Testing- Microbiology: A variety of options for only bacteria testing.
- HUD/FHA Mortgage Testing: Testing required by HUD for a HUD/FHA loan.
