Private Well Water Testing

This page has been automatically translated from English. MSDH has not reviewed this translation and is not responsible for any inaccuracies.

The Mississipi State Department of Health tests private wells for bacteriological contamination only. Testing for mineral content, chemicals or other contaminants should be done through a private laboratory.

This is a sampling service for private water wells only. For homes connected to public water systems, contact your water supply operator, or MSDH's Bureau of Public Water Supply if you have concerns about your water quality.

  • Apply online for private well water sampling and testing
  • Private water testing laboratories participating in MSDH's Drinking Water Lab Certification Program
