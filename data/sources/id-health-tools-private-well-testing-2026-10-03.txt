Free well water testing for Idaho families. Is your drinking water from a private well? The Idaho Department of Health and Welfare is offering free private well water testing while funding lasts. Testing is important in ensuring your well water is safe to drink.
To receive the free well water testing, you are agreeing to share test results on the Idaho Ground Water Near Me Map. Homeowner information, such as name and address will not be displayed on the map. Once your request for the free water testing has been submitted, a representative will be reaching out to you with next steps.
Contact the Idaho Department of Health and Welfare Environmental Health Program if you have additional questions at 800-445-8647 or eh@dhw.idaho.gov.
*Only one test kit per household can be ordered every five years.*
Note: Materials will NOT be shipped outside the State of Idaho.
