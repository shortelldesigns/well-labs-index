# Archive note — VDH Private Well Program
URL: https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/environmental-health/private-well-program/
Retrieved: 2026-09-11 via WebFetch (curl returned HTTP 403)

Key facts:
- Start with LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENT (Environmental Health) for permits/construction.
- VDH does NOT test private wells. Operation and maintenance are the well owner's responsibility.
- VDH does not have authority to test private well quality or require others to test (except new-well coliform under Private Well Regulations 12VAC5-630).
- After installation, subsequent testing is at the discretion of the well user.
- Home-purchase testing is not required by VDH but may be required by lenders.
