Key facts:
- DCLS does not provide consultation on private well testing or testing for homeowners on a public water supply.
- DCLS drinking water analysis is for public water supply systems / regulated waterworks.
- For DCLS-certified/accredited drinking water labs, use Find a Lab listings.
