                              DRINKING WATER

  The Department of Public Health can only accept laboratory analyses of drinking water samples toward fulfillment of
  the monitoring requirements in Section 19-13-B102 of the Public Health Code if the results are from an approved
  laboratory. The following list includes laboratories in Connecticut only. Information on out of State laboratories can be
  acquired directly from the Approval Program at DPH by calling (860) 509-7389 or by accessing the website at
  www.ct.gov/dph/environmentallabs. Also, you may call the aforementioned phone number to verify the parameters
  within each contaminant's group for which the laboratories are certified.



  All commercial labs on this list are approved to test drinking water; however only some of these labs offer testing
  services for private wells.
 The following is an explanation used in the laboratory listing:



        Private Well Testing are laboratories that accept samples from private wells.

        Bacteriological are some or all of the following: Total Coliform and E. Coli

        Inorganics are some or all of the following: Inorganic Chemicals, Physical, Sanitary Chemicals, Nitrate, Metals

        Organic Chemicals are some or all of the following: Semi-volatile Compounds, Haloacetic Acids, Volatile
        Organic Compounds, Pesticides, PCBs, Synthetic Organic Compounds

        PFAS are Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances

        Radiological are some or all of the following: Gross Alpha, Gross Beta, Radium, Radon, Uranium

        Uranium by EPA Method 200.8
