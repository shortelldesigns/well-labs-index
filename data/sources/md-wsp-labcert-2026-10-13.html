Maryland’s Drinking Water Lab Certification Program

Main_Content


MDE’s Laboratory Certification Program (LCP) is the accreditation body for all certified drinking water (DW) laboratories in the State of Maryland, ensuring labs meet standards set by the Safe Drinking Water Act  (SDWA).  


These labs have met the minimum criteria specified under federal & State regulations listed under the Laws and Regulations  section below.  For specific questions regarding laboratory certification, consult the Laboratory Certification Guide (Permit Guide 3.17) . 

Only state-certified labs are permitted to analyze compliance samples for Maryland’s Public Water Systems (PWS).  In addition, compliance samples must be collected by a state certified sampler.  For more information on sampler certification requirements, please refer to the Water Sampler Certification  section below.


For private residential, real estate, and other non-compliance testing, we recommend using a certified sampler to ensure reliable test results.

Water Sampler Certification (via MRWA)

Performing the required testing of public water systems is the primary means of evaluating the safety of the drinking water supply. This begins with the proper collection of samples.

In the State of Maryland, all compliance samples under the SDWA are REQUIRED to be collected by an active, certified sampler.  

*Important note* – The Maryland Rural Water Association (MRWA) is the only approved training provider in the state.  Contact & registration information can be found under Fact Sheet link below.

Laboratory Certification Applications and Forms

APPLICATIONS FOR DW LAB CERTIFICATION IN THE STATE OF MARYLAND:

  • New Laboratory Application – Use for new In-State labs and dissolved labs looking to be reinstated for DW lab certification in Maryland.  
  • In-State Renewal Application – Use for In-State Labs requesting to maintain existing certification for Maryland (required annually).
  • Out-of-State Renewal Application – Use for Out-of-State labs requesting to maintain certification (required annually) and new Out-of-State labs looking to be certified in Maryland.

FORMS ASSOCIATED WITH THE LAB CERTIFICATION PROCESS ABOVE:

  • Lab Organization & Personnel Qualifications Form – Provides LCP with list of required organization personnel for the relevant lab operations, along with their pertinent experience.
  • Supervisory Level Personnel Registration Form – Provides LCP required information regarding new supervisory level personnel.
  • Analyst Registration Form (In-State labs only) – Provides LCP required information regarding new analysts/technicians.
  • Requested Test Method List (Note: download using Chrome browser only) – Allows laboratories to select individually all requested test methods for certification (retaining from prior year’s certification or adding) and select deleted parameters (for renewals) from prior year.
  • Payment Coupon Form – Allows our program with a means of tracking payments for annual renewal and new / initial lab certification fee.
